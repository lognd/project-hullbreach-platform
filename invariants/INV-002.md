---
id: INV-002
statement: Every unauthenticated request field that reaches a hasher, a parser
  or an in-process store is length-bounded, and every in-process store keyed by
  request data has a bounded key count.
criticality: high
evidence:
  - ticket 01M4GR03P2MAC7ASRP2XGJV9R3
  - ticket 01M4GR0CQQA02HKSTK8TDEV1SB
  - ticket 01M4GR6E7SMM7QSA1VWV1ATJQ2
---

Rationale: RegisterRequest.password has min_length but no max_length, and
LoginRequest has no bounds at all, so a multi-megabyte password is fed to
Argon2id (CPU and memory DoS); _failed_attempts is keyed by attacker-chosen
usernames and never evicts empty deques (memory DoS).
